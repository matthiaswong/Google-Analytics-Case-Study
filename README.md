This is a case study analysing the causation and relationship between several independent factors such as: 

- Shooting Ability 
- Free Throws Shot 
- Defensive Ability
(to name a few)

of NCAA teams over the past 10 years and their ability to achieve success, which is measured by: 

their ability to make the NCAA playoffs. (The dependent Variable)

It is noted that based on the single linear regression models created: 
**The greatest contributor or difference-maker between a playoff and non-playoff team is the difference in the two point shot percentage**

In the RMD file, I make several suggestions to teams who are struggling to make the playoffs; such as: 

Increasing training intensity on two-point shots 

This is while noting that the dataset is limited in it's sample size and that it is difficult to map a multi regression model to account for the true difference between a playoff and non-playoff team.
